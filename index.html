Ilona Ayelén Pajor

Political scientist, university lecturer and gender studies researcher

I hold a bachelor's degree in Political Science and Government (UCES) and specialisations in Public Policies and Gender Justice (CLACSO/FLACSO) and in University Teaching (UNNE). I am currently pursuing a Master's in Gender Studies and Policies at UNTREF, where I am a member of the research group Structural Violence, Spaces and Social Subjects and develop my own research line on FemTech, exploring the links between technology, gender and cognitive capitalism. I combine research with teaching: I am an adjunct professor at UCES, where I teach the courses Political Economy and Economics and Creative Industries.

Interests: technology and gender (FemTech), gender public policy, gender-based violence and university teaching.
